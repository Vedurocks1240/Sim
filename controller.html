Connect in 4 steps

  1. On your computer, open geo-fs.com with the extension installed. A QR code appears in the bottom-right corner.
  2. Point your phone camera at the QR code and open the link. This page opens and connects by itself.
  3. If the status says connected ✔, turn the phone to landscape and switch off auto-rotate lock if the layout looks wrong.
  4. For gyroscope steering, tap Gyro, allow motion access when asked, then hold the phone level and tap Calibrate in ⚙ Settings.

Troubleshooting

  • Stuck on “connecting”: put both devices on the same Wi-Fi. Guest, hotel or office networks often block direct connections.
  • Gyro does nothing: iPhone needs Safari and a tap on “Allow”; Android needs Chrome over HTTPS. Check Settings → Safari → Motion & Orientation Access.
  • Axis is reversed: open ⚙ and use the Invert switches.
  • Connection dropped: reload this page and scan the QR again. The code changes each time GeoFS is reloaded.
